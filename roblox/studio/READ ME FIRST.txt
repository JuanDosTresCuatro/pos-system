RoPOS: a point of sale system for Roblox
=========================================

Staff ring up orders at a till in your game. The customer approves the
payment on their own screen, the money moves, stock goes down and the items
appear in their backpack. Managers get shift reports, refunds, price and
stock control, 7-day reports and an audit log. Prices are in pounds (£).

All you need is Roblox Studio (free from create.roblox.com).


WHAT IS IN THIS FOLDER
----------------------

RoPOS.rbxlx      A complete demo game. Open it and press Play.
Models/          The 3 parts of RoPOS, for adding it to your own game.
Scripts/         The same code as plain files, laid out like Studio's
                 Explorer, if you want to read it. You do not need these
                 to use RoPOS.


OPTION 1: TRY THE DEMO (2 minutes)
----------------------------------

1. Double-click RoPOS.rbxlx (or in Studio: File > Open from File).
2. Press Play (F5). A Blox Mart shop counter appears in front of you.
3. Walk round the back of the counter and hold E on the till.
4. Go to the Shift tab and press "Open shift".
5. On the Sell tab, tap some items, press "Choose customer", pick
   yourself and press "Charge".
6. Your payment screen pops up. Press "Pay". The items appear in your
   backpack and your Cash (top right) goes down.

In Studio play tests everyone is a manager and you can charge yourself,
so you can try everything alone. To test a cashier and a customer
separately, use Test > Clients and Servers with 2 players.


OPTION 2: ADD IT TO YOUR OWN GAME (5 minutes)
---------------------------------------------

1. Open your game in Studio.
2. In the Explorer, right-click each service below and choose
   "Insert from File...", then pick the matching file in Models/:

     Right-click this                    Insert this file
     ----------------------------------  ------------------------
     ReplicatedStorage                   Models/POS.rbxmx
     ServerScriptService                 Models/POSServer.rbxmx
     StarterPlayer > StarterPlayerScripts   Models/POSClient.rbxmx

   Keep the names POS, POSServer and POSClient exactly as they are.

3. Press Play. If your game has no till yet, RoPOS builds a demo counter
   so you can test straight away.

4. Open ReplicatedStorage > POS > Config to set up your shop:
   - Config.Store: shop name, receipt prefix, receipt message
   - Config.Products: your items, prices (whole pounds), stock
   - Config.Currency: starting money, or use your game's own leaderstats
   - Config.Staff: who your cashiers and managers are (see below)
   Every setting is explained in the file.


USE YOUR OWN TILL
-----------------

1. Build a till model (or use a single Part).
2. Select it, open Properties > Tags and add the tag:  POSRegister
3. Optional attributes on the model:
     RegisterName (string)  for example "Drive-thru"
     RegisterId   (string)  a fixed ID if you have several tills
4. Optional: put a Part named "Screen" inside the model. Its front face
   becomes the screen customers see. Without one, a floating screen
   appears above the till.
5. In Config, set Demo.SpawnRegister = false so the demo counter is not built.


GIVE CUSTOMERS THE ITEMS THEY BUY
---------------------------------

Put a Tool in ServerStorage > POSItems with the same name as the product's
Tool setting in Config.Products (for example a Tool named "Bloxy Cola").
RoPOS gives a copy to the customer for every one they buy. Until you add
your own, it creates simple placeholder Tools.


STAFF: WHO CAN USE THE TILL
---------------------------

Customers can only buy. Cashiers can sell, give small discounts (up to
10%) and open and close shifts. Managers can also refund, change prices
and stock, and read reports and the audit log.

Set this before you publish, in Config.Staff:
  - GroupId, CashierRank, ManagerRank: use your Roblox group's ranks, or
  - Users: list user IDs, for example  [123456789] = "Manager"
The owner of the game is always a manager.

Studio gives everyone the manager role for testing. To test the real
rules in Studio, set Config.Staff.StudioRole = nil.


SAVING DATA
-----------

In a published game, RoPOS saves money, stock, sales and reports in
Roblox DataStores, and every server of the game shares them.

Studio does not save anything until you:
  1. Publish the game to Roblox (File > Publish to Roblox), and
  2. Turn on Game Settings > Security > "Enable Studio Access to API Services".
Until then the POS shows "Studio: data not saved" and everything resets
when you stop the test.


IF SOMETHING DOES NOT WORK
--------------------------

- Nothing happens when I hold E: check you are a cashier or manager
  (see Staff above) and standing within about 2.5 m of the till.
- "needs to stand at the register": the customer must be within 16 studs
  (about 4.5 m) of the till.
- The POS window closes by itself: the cashier walked more than 20 studs
  (about 5.6 m) from the till.
- Errors in the Output window (View > Output) start with [RoPOS] and say
  what to fix.
